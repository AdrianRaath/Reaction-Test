## The average reaction time at every age

The best age-by-age data comes from lab studies that measure large samples on identical equipment. In one of the largest, 1,469 adults took the same simple reaction test, and every age group from 18 to 65 landed inside a 21-millisecond band.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

<AgeCurve />

The slide is steady and small, roughly half a millisecond per year.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> A UK survey of 7,130 adults spanning ages 18 to 82 refined the shape: simple reaction time holds close to flat until about 50, and the slowing picks up from there, while tasks that add a decision to the reaction slow across the whole adult range.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup> Decisions age earlier than reflexes do.

Two cautions before you compare yourself to these numbers. They come from optimized lab equipment; a browser test reads higher because your screen, mouse, and browser add their own delay, which is why typical online medians sit near 273 ms across all ages.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup> And an average is a midpoint, not a floor: healthy people of the same age differ from each other by more than the entire 21 ms age spread.

## When reaction time peaks, and when it fades

Children start slow. Reaction speed climbs through childhood and adolescence as the brain's processing quickens, and a meta-analysis of 72 studies found the gains continuing into young adulthood.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

The peak arrives early. In a study of 3,305 StarCraft II players aged 16 to 44, within-game reaction speed began its decline at age 24.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup> The lab data agrees on the direction: 18-to-24 is the fastest cohort, and each later group runs a touch slower.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> From there the fade stays gentle until it steepens past 50.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## Age is not the whole story

The StarCraft study's second finding matters more than its first. Older players held their ground in real matches by compensating for slower reactions with better use of shortcuts and command keys: less raw speed, more efficiency.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup> Experience is a genuine performance asset, and it keeps compounding long after 24.

The age effect is also small next to the state you show up in. Four decades of aging costs about 21 ms.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> One sleepless night impairs you like being over the drink-drive limit, a badly timed coffee bills your next day, and a laggy device adds tens of milliseconds on its own. Your habits move your number further than your birth year does. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good reaction time for my age?

Lab averages move from about 218 ms in the early 20s to 239 ms in the early 60s.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> Browser tests read higher because devices add delay, with online medians near 273 ms across all ages.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup> In a browser, an average around 250 ms is strong for any adult, and beating 273 ms beats the typical tester regardless of your decade.

### At what age is reaction time fastest?

The late teens and early twenties. The 18 to 24 cohort is the fastest group in large lab samples,<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> a study of 3,305 esports players put the start of the decline at age 24,<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup> and children are still climbing toward adult speed through adolescence.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Why is my reaction time slower than the averages here?

Check the measurement before the reflexes. Phones and slow monitors add 35 to 140 ms to a score, and a browser number is not comparable to a lab number.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup> Sleep, caffeine timing, and focus swing results by more than the whole age effect. And an average is a midpoint, so half of every age group sits below it.
