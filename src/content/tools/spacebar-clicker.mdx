<section id="what-is" class="info-section">
  <div class="container">
    <h2>What is Spacebar Clicker?</h2>

      Spacebar Clicker is an idle game played on one key. Every press of the spacebar feeds energy
      into a lab reactor. You spend that energy on upgrades that make each press worth more, and on
      auto-tappers that press for you while you do something else. The reactor changes state as
      you earn, and once a run has produced a million energy you can reset the experiment for
      isotopes: a permanent bonus, and new rules that unlock one at a time.

      The difference from other spacebar clicker games is the combo. Most clickers reward mashing.
      This one tracks the gap between your presses and pays up to three times more energy when you
      hold a steady beat. A player tapping five times a second in rhythm out-earns a frantic masher
      by a wide margin, which makes it a cousin of the <a href="/spacebar-speed-test">spacebar speed
      test</a> rather than a copy of it.

      Perks add the luck. They drop into the shop at random while you play, and each one bends a
      rule for the rest of the run, so one run might lean on rhythm and the next on machines.

      Everything saves in your browser. No account, no ads, no download.

  </div>
</section>

    <h2>How to play Spacebar Clicker</h2>
    <ol class="steps-list section-content">
      <li>
        <strong>Press the spacebar</strong>

          Each press adds energy to the reactor. On a phone, tap the reactor instead. The first
          upgrade costs {PRESS_UPGRADES[0].baseCost} energy, so a steady tapper can afford it within
          fifteen seconds.

      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Hold a rhythm</strong>

          Tap at a pace you can keep. After ten presses in time your combo reaches Steady and every
          press pays 1.5 times as much. Keep going for Locked in, Resonance and Harmonic. The bar
          under the combo counter drains during the 0.8 second grace window, so you can see a
          combo about to break. Below it, a trace draws one spike per press. Evenly spaced spikes
          mean you are on the beat, and a short spike marks a press that missed it.

      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Buy upgrades and auto-tappers</strong>

          Finger Strength and Spring Keys raise what a press is worth. Lab Assistants, Pneumatic
          Keys and the rest generate energy on their own, even while you are away. New auto-tappers
          appear in the shop once a run has earned half their price.

      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Mash the surge</strong>

          Every few minutes of active play the reactor warns you, counts down from three, and then
          pays ten times per press for five seconds. This is the one moment speed beats rhythm. The
          game records your presses per second for each surge.

      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Catch the perks</strong>

          Now and then a perk drops into the shop: a random bonus that lasts the rest of the run.
          A ring around it drains as you earn, so decide before it runs out. You can hold up to
          {PERK_SLOTS} at once, and they show in the reactor's top-right corner.

      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Reset the experiment</strong>

          Once a run has earned a million energy you can reset it for isotopes. Each isotope adds
          10% to every gain for good, and unlocks a rung of the isotope track: a head start, a
          longer surge, a sixth perk slot and more. Your energy, shop, reactor state and perks
          start over; your isotopes, achievements, records and perk codex stay. Isotopes sit as
          beads in the reactor's top-left corner: point at them or tap them for their bonus and
          the next unlock. Once a reset is open, a ring beside them shows what it would pay.

      </li>
    </ol>
    <div class="note">
      <strong>Note:</strong> Holding the spacebar down does nothing. The game ignores the repeat
      events your operating system fires for a held key, and it discards any press that lands
      within 33 milliseconds of the one before.
    </div>

</section>

<section id="combo" class="info-section">
  <div class="container">
    <h2>The combo: steady beats fast</h2>

      A press keeps rhythm when the gap since the last press is within 25% of the average of your
      previous five gaps, and under 0.6 seconds. Each press that keeps rhythm adds one to the combo.
      The first five presses after a break count regardless, so a combo can start. One or two
      off-beat presses are forgiven and add nothing. The third in a row breaks the combo, and so
      does a pause longer than 0.8 seconds.

    <div class="spec-table spec-table-3 section-content">
      <div class="spec-row spec-head">
        <span class="spec-name">Combo</span>
        <span class="spec-num">Multiplier</span>
        <span class="spec-desc">Tier</span>
      </div>
      {tiers.map((tier, i) => (
        <div class="spec-row">
          <span class="spec-name">{tierRange(i)}</span>
          <span class="spec-num">x{tier.multiplier}</span>
          <span class="spec-desc">{tier.label || 'No tier yet'}</span>
        </div>
      ))}
    </div>

      The combo multiplies your presses only. Auto-tappers ignore it until you own Synchronisation,
      which gives them half of your current multiplier. Rhythm, not speed, is what the tracker
      measures: a tapper at three presses a second holds a combo as well as one at eight, as long as
      the gaps stay even.

      Two perks change this table for a run. Overtone adds a tier above Harmonic, and Glass Rhythm
      multiplies every tier by two to three but lets two off-beat presses in a row break the
      combo.

</section>

<section id="prestige" class="info-section">
  <div class="container">
    <h2>Reset the experiment</h2>

      Once a run has earned a million energy, the Isotopes tab lets you reset the experiment: the
      run starts over, and you gain isotopes. Isotopes gained equal the square root of your run's
      energy in millions, rounded down: one million gives 1, four million gives 2, twenty-five
      million gives 5, a hundred million gives 10. Each isotope adds 10% to every gain, presses and auto-tappers
      alike, on every run from then on. Your perks go with the reset, unless the isotope track
      lets you keep one, and the codex of perks you have found stays.

      The thresholds climb, but a run keeps speeding up as it goes, so each isotope after the first
      arrives sooner than the one before. In the game's simulation, a steady player with no perks
      earns the first isotope at about 24 minutes and the second about 12 minutes later. The
      isotope beads and the Isotopes tab show what a reset gives now and how far off
      the next isotope is, so when to reset is your call, and the game asks you to confirm.

    <h3 id="isotope-track">The isotope track</h3>

      Each isotope you hold also unlocks a rung of the isotope track, for good. Nothing is spent:
      holding the isotopes is enough, and a reset that pays several unlocks several at once. Most
      rungs are small, like a head start on every run or a longer surge. Every third rung is a
      choice of two, which you make in the reset dialog and can change at any reset. The Isotopes
      tab shows the rungs you hold and the next two, and the game names a rung when it
      shapes an offer, a surge or your time away.
