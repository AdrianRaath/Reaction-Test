faq:
  - question: 'Is Spacebar Clicker free to play?'
    answer: 'Yes. Spacebar Clicker is a free browser game with no download and no account. Open the page, press the spacebar, and you are playing.'
  - question: 'Does my progress save?'
    answer: 'Yes. The game saves to your browser every five seconds and again when you leave the page, so you can close the tab and pick up where you stopped. The save lives on this device in this browser. Clearing site data erases it, and the Reset button in the toolbar wipes it on purpose.'
  - question: 'Can I play Spacebar Clicker on my phone?'
    answer: 'Yes. Tap the reactor instead of pressing a key. Taps build the combo the same way key presses do, and auto-tappers run the same.'
  - question: 'Does Spacebar Clicker work at school or work?'
    answer: 'It is an ordinary web page with no download or plugin, so it runs anywhere a browser can reach reflexlab.co. Whether a school or office network allows the site is up to whoever runs that network, and nothing on this page can change that.'
  - question: 'Can I use an auto clicker?'
    answer: 'You can, but the game throws away any press that arrives less than 33 milliseconds after the last one, so anything above 30 presses per second is wasted. Nothing bans you. The game is built around holding your own rhythm, and auto-tappers already exist to do the automating for you.'
  - question: 'How is Spacebar Clicker different from the spacebar speed test?'
    answer: 'The spacebar speed test measures your presses per second over a fixed window and gives you a rank. Spacebar Clicker is an idle game: progress carries over between visits, presses buy upgrades, and a steady rhythm matters more than raw speed. A surge is the one moment the game asks you to mash, and it records your presses per second like the test does.'
  - question: 'What does holding a rhythm do?'
    answer: 'Each press that lands within 25% of your recent average gap adds one to your combo. At 10 combo every press is worth 1.5 times as much, at 25 it is doubled, at 50 it is 2.5 times and at 100 it is tripled. Pause for more than 0.8 seconds, or miss the beat three times in a row, and the combo breaks.'
  - question: 'When can I reset for the first time?'
    answer: 'Once a run has earned a million energy. A steady combo, the upgrades you choose and the perks you buy all bring that closer. The Isotopes tab shows how far off it is.'
  - question: 'What happens when I leave the game?'
    answer: 'Auto-tappers keep earning at full output for 30 minutes after your last press or purchase. Over the next 90 minutes the output falls to nothing, and at two hours the reactor faults. One press brings back a reactor that is running down, and ten presses on a steady beat restart one that has faulted. Closing the tab follows the same rule.'
  - question: 'How do perks work?'
    answer: 'Perks are random drops that change the rules for one run. One appears at the top of the Upgrades or Auto-tappers tab while you play, and leaves once you have earned past its window, so buy the ones you want before the ring around it empties. About two drops in three are commons that boost a number, three in ten are rares that change a rule, and one in twenty is a keystone that breaks a rule at a cost. You hold up to five, one keystone at a time, and they clear when you reset. The isotope track can raise both limits and carry one perk through a reset.'
  - question: 'What does the isotope track unlock?'
    answer: 'Each isotope you hold unlocks one rung of the track, for good, and you spend nothing. The first starts each run with 500 energy and a Lab Assistant. Later rungs lengthen surges and improve perk drops and time away, and every third rung is a choice of two, such as a sixth perk slot or perks that drop at level II. You pick in the reset dialog and can switch at any reset. The track has twelve rungs.'
  - question: 'Does Spacebar Clicker have an end?'
    answer: 'No. The isotope track stops at twelve rungs, and isotopes past the twelfth still add 10% each. The game also keeps achievements, your fastest run to a million, and a codex of all 25 perks to fill.'

export const RARITY_ORDER = { common: 0, rare: 1, keystone: 2 };
export const perksOf = (family) =>
  PERKS.filter((p) => p.family === family).sort(
    (a, b) => RARITY_ORDER[a.rarity] - RARITY_ORDER[b.rarity]
  );
export const levels = (p) => p.values.map((v) => formatPerkValue(p.unit, v)).join(' / ');
export const RARITIES = ['common', 'rare', 'keystone'];
export const tiers = [...COMBO_TIERS].reverse();

export const SHOP_ITEMS = [...PRESS_UPGRADES, ...AUTO_TAPPERS];
export const list = (names) =>
  names.length < 2 ? names.join('') : `${names.slice(0, -1).join(', ')} and ${names.at(-1)}`;
export const stateNote = (s, i) => {
  const next = REACTOR_STATES[i + 1]?.threshold ?? Infinity;
  const gain = prestigeGain(s.threshold);
  if (gain === 1) return 'A reset opens, for 1 isotope';
  if (gain > 1) return `A reset pays ${gain} isotopes`;
  const names = SHOP_ITEMS.filter(
    (item) => item.baseCost >= s.threshold && item.baseCost < next
  ).map((item) => item.name);
  return `First prices in reach: ${list(names)}`;
};
export const LAMPS = REACTOR_STATES.slice(1).map((_, k, all) => {
  const angle = (k / all.length) * 2 * Math.PI - Math.PI / 2;
  return [(50 + 46 * Math.cos(angle)).toFixed(1), (50 + 46 * Math.sin(angle)).toFixed(1)];
});
export const fullMin = RUNDOWN.fullS / 60;
export const fallMin = RUNDOWN.fallS / 60;
export const faultH = (RUNDOWN.fullS + RUNDOWN.fallS) / 3600;
export const mostMin = fullMin + fallMin / 2;
export const restartMarks = Array.from(
  { length: RESTART_PRESSES },
  (_, i) => `M${526 + i * 9} 120V${112 - i * (90 / (RESTART_PRESSES - 1))}`

export const nightShift = PERKS.find((p) => p.id === 'night-shift').values;
export const millions = (v) => `${v / 1_000_000}M`;
export const TRACK_ROWS = [0, 3, 6, 9].map((i) => ISOTOPE_TRACK.slice(i, i + 3));
export const tierRange = (i) =>
  i === tiers.length - 1 ? `${tiers[i].min}+` : `${tiers[i].min} to ${tiers[i + 1].min - 1}`;

<section id="faq" class="info-section">
  <div class="container">
    <h2>FAQ about this Spacebar Clicker</h2>
    <div class="faq-list section-content">
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Is Spacebar Clicker free to play?</summary>

          Yes. Spacebar Clicker is a free browser game with no download and no account.
          Open the page, press the spacebar, and you are playing.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Does my progress save?</summary>

          Yes. The game saves to your browser every five seconds and again when you leave
          the page, so you can close the tab and pick up where you stopped. The save lives
          on this device in this browser. Clearing site data erases it, and the Reset
          button in the toolbar wipes it on purpose.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Can I play Spacebar Clicker on my phone?</summary>

          Yes. Tap the reactor instead of pressing a key. Taps build the combo the same
          way key presses do, and auto-tappers run the same.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Does Spacebar Clicker work at school or work?</summary>

          It is an ordinary web page with no download or plugin, so it runs anywhere a
          browser can reach reflexlab.co. Whether a school or office network allows the
          site is up to whoever runs that network, and nothing on this page can change
          that.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Can I use an auto clicker?</summary>

          You can, but the game throws away any press that arrives less than 33
          milliseconds after the last one, so anything above 30 presses per second is
          wasted. Nothing bans you. The game is built around holding your own rhythm, and
          auto-tappers already exist to do the automating for you.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>How is Spacebar Clicker different from the spacebar speed test?</summary>

          The <a href="/spacebar-speed-test">spacebar speed test</a> measures your presses
          per second over a fixed window and gives you a rank. Spacebar Clicker is an idle
          game: progress carries over between visits, presses buy upgrades, and a steady
          rhythm matters more than raw speed. A surge is the one moment the game asks you
          to mash, and it records your presses per second like the test does.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>What does holding a rhythm do?</summary>

          Each press that lands within 25% of your recent average gap adds one to your
          combo. At 10 combo every press is worth 1.5 times as much, at 25 it is doubled,
          at 50 it is 2.5 times and at 100 it is tripled. Pause for more than 0.8 seconds,
          or miss the beat three times in a row, and the combo breaks.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>When can I reset for the first time?</summary>

          Once a run has earned a million energy. A steady combo, the upgrades you choose
          and the perks you buy all bring that closer. The Isotopes tab shows how far off
          it is.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>What happens when I leave the game?</summary>

          Auto-tappers keep earning at full output for 30 minutes after your last press or
          purchase. Over the next 90 minutes the output falls to nothing, and at two hours
          the reactor faults. One press brings back a reactor that is running down, and
          ten presses on a steady beat restart one that has faulted. Closing the tab
          follows the same rule, which the <a href="#rundown">time away section</a> draws.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>How do perks work?</summary>

          Perks are random drops that change the rules for one run. One appears at the top
          of the Upgrades or Auto-tappers tab while you play, and leaves once you have
          earned past its window, so buy the ones you want before the ring around it
          empties. About two drops in three are commons that boost a number, three in ten
          are rares that change a rule, and one in twenty is a keystone that breaks a rule
          at a cost. You hold up to five, one keystone at a time, and they clear when you
          reset. The isotope track can raise both limits and carry one perk through a
          reset. The <a href="#perks">perks section</a> lists all 25.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>What does the isotope track unlock?</summary>

          Each isotope you hold unlocks one rung of the track, for good, and you spend
          nothing. The first starts each run with 500 energy and a Lab Assistant. Later
          rungs lengthen surges and improve perk drops and time away, and every third rung
          is a choice of two, such as a sixth perk slot or perks that drop at level II.
          You pick in the reset dialog and can switch at any reset. The track has twelve
          rungs, and the <a href="#isotope-track">isotope track section</a> lists them.

      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Does Spacebar Clicker have an end?</summary>

          No. The isotope track stops at twelve rungs, and isotopes past the twelfth still
          add 10% each. The game also keeps achievements, your fastest run to a million,
          and a codex of all 25 perks to fill.

      </details>
    </div>

  </div>
</section>
